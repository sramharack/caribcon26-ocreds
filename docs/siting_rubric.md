# Siting framework – scoring anchors (expert-elicited prototype)

Scores run from 1 to 5, unweighted. A score of 3 means the constraint exists but conventional mitigation can address it.

| Factor | 5 | 1 | TT | JM-KGN | JM-OH | JM-MB |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Grid headroom | Large reserve margin; DC <1% of peak | Tight margin; DC >5% of peak | 5 | 3 | 4 | 2 |
| RE resource quality | Solar CF ≥0.21 or wind CF ≥0.30 nearby | Weak resource / little land | 3 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| Transmission adequacy | HV substation on site, uncongested | New HV line or congested corridor | 5 | 4 | 4 | 2 |
| Submarine fibre RTT | <30 ms to Miami, diverse cables | >60 ms or single cable | 4 | 5 | 3 | 3 |
| IX/peering proximity | IXP in same metro | No domestic IXP nearby | 3 | 4 | 1 | 2 |
| Hurricane exposure | Rarely on track; outside surge zone | Frequent major-storm track; coastal | 5 | 3 | 3 | 2 |
| Water availability | Unstressed or non-potable source | Stressed municipal supply only | 4 | 3 | 4 | 3 |
| Seismic risk | Low hazard zone | High hazard; near active fault | 4 | 3 | 3 | 3 |
| Noise buffer feasibility | ≥150 m (~500 ft) to residences feasible | Residences <50 m | 4 | 2 | 4 | 3 |
| Zoning readiness | Heavy-industrial zoning permits DC | Rezoning + full EIA needed | 4 | 3 | 3 | 2 |
